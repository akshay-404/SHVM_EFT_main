# PLB Letter and PRD flavour consistency revision, v11

The Letter is adapted for a focused PLB submission; the longer SHVM construction
is prepared separately for PRD. The two manuscripts now agree on one physical
neutral-scalar LFV witness and on the conditional status of channel comparisons.
No journal submission has been made. Journal acceptance is not certified.

## Scientific changes

1. Closest prior comparison: tree flavon LFV and scalar/pseudoscalar cancellations
   are established (Pascoli-Zhou); dipoles/off-shell scalar photon forms are
   established (Harnik-Kopp-Zupan, Hung et al.). Completeness invariance and the
   amplitude triangle inequality are standard. The Letter's addition is the
   quantified SHVM alignment domain, finite-timelike interference in that domain,
   and a rescaled counterexample. It claims no new loop function or naturalness theorem.
2. A flavour-specific neutral-Yukawa local-box majorant replaces the earlier
   unqualified dimensional estimate. Entrywise chiral vertex sums, positive
   Euclidean integrals, mass-numerator inequalities and an intentionally loose
   topology/Fierz count give W_B<=3.50954e-14 GeV^-2. The integrated norm bound
   is 0.08949 of the central three-body amplitude. This applies to leading local
   neutral-Yukawa boxes only; it is not a finite complete-parent box calculation.
3. The Higgs/top input is consistently Ytt=172.5/246, with MH=125 GeV.
   Absolute top/W-photon terms define a 3% dipole budget without their cancellation.
   Sufficient angle caps (h-s1,h-s2,h-s4,h-s5) are approximately
   (2.60e-7,6.45e-6,2.48e-8,1.09e-7) radians. They are conservative matching choices,
   not measured angle limits. General direct Higgs LFV and non-SM Higgs couplings
   require new bounds. Remaining weak and mediator operators are explicitly budgeted.
4. The sufficient enclosure combines boxes, loop-sized extra local disks, tiny
   Higgs admixture and residual norm requirements (0.075 in three-body amplitude,
   0.05 in dipole). The totals are 0.23730 and 0.08897; rounded caps 0.25/0.10
   give R in [0.086412,0.358567]. This is a conditional deterministic enclosure,
   not a prior, confidence interval or total inclusive NLO theoretical uncertainty.
5. A positive source-supported tree potential demonstrates coexistence of small
   VEVs and heavy nearly aligned poles. It does not prove radiative closure of
   all flavour poles. The collective-Higgs symmetry is not imported as such a proof.
6. The longer paper's abstract, introduction, result tables, LFV application,
   figures, conclusion and reproduction instructions now use B3e=4.34750e-15
   and R=0.185881. Contact-only (4.02901e-15 massive), contact-plus-dipole
   (3.92895e-15) and older suppression examples are explicitly subset diagnostics.

## Common numbers and experimental meaning

Beg=2.3388581e-14; B3e=4.3474999e-15; R=0.1858813 at all eight physical
poles and Lambda_D equal to 5 TeV, canonical residues, no extra matching or Higgs mixing.
Physical pole lepton masses enter loops/phase space; the six-decimal texture SVD
couplings remain the prescribed boundary, not a new pole-mass precision fit.
The local controlled-domain enclosure gives B3e in [2.44547,6.79297]e-15 and
Beg in [1.89448,2.83002]e-14. These muon rates satisfy the quoted present limits,
but the B3e range straddles the idealized phase-I 90% exclusion threshold.
Central phase-I SES and idealized zero-event 90% reaches are 5.510 and 4.964 TeV;
MEG design median 90% reach is 4.425 TeV. A central reach is not guaranteed over
the matching domain. Delta=0.05 at 10 TeV gives Beg=4.91e-14, B3e=3.06e-16 and
R=0.00623: an allowed-muon-rate counterexample, not a global viability point.

## Verification and remaining scope

Finite-photon spin sums, Ward identity, antiparticle completeness, Fierz identities,
contact/dipole normalization and integration convergence pass. The additional
same-chirality antisymmetrized tensor=-12 scalar check passes at <4e-16 relative.
Euclidean integral and mass-numerator inequalities are checked independently.
The flavour charge/anomaly and isolated dynamical-bridge audits pass in their
stated scope. The core quark/neutrino construction is retained; no new full
quark likelihood, nonperturbative spectrum or experimental covariance is invented.
The two copies of the current LFV boundary and physical code are identical.

Unknown strong/weak parent coefficients are not determined by the mass texture.
The residual budgets need verification in any parent used to claim viability.
Inclusive QED running/real-virtual corrections and a full EW/global fit are not
completed; no percent-level total rate or broad model-viability claim is made.
The separate Paper I and spectral manuscripts are unchanged v9 companions.
